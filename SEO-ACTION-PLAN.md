Post 3 — opinion
> **Sometimes the right website is one page.**
> A ten-page site with nine pages nobody reads does not rank better than one page that answers everything. What ranks is clarity: what you do, who it is for, where you work, and how to reach you. If your site has fourteen pages and three conversions a year, the problem is not the page count.

> **Three signs it is time, none of them about looks.**
> 1. You cannot edit your own hours without calling someone.
> 2. It takes more than three seconds to load on a phone.
> 3. Someone searches your business name and a directory outranks your own site.
> Any one of those is worth fixing before a redesign for style.

Post 6 — industries

### Reviews — the multiplier
Ask **every** past client, the same week the work finishes. Target 10 reviews in 90 days; the local pack responds to review count and recency more than to anything else you can control.

> Hi [name] — the site's been live a couple of weeks now, hope it's working well. If you've got two minutes, a short Google review would genuinely help other local businesses find us. Here's the direct link: [review link]. No pressure either way.

Reply to every review — including the critical ones — within a few days.

> **Risk to fix first:** `GBP-Optimization-Pack.md` contains a post quoting "J. Henley Customs" saying they went from page 4 to page 1. Unless that is a real, documented review, do not publish it. Google suspends profiles for fabricated reviews, and it is a false advertising claim in Canada. The same applies to the three testimonials on the homepage (Wanita, Samira, Vince) — confirm each is a real quote with permission, or replace them.

## 3. Order of work

**Week 1** — GBP: categories, description, services, 10 photos, 5 Q&A, first post. Bing Places
import. Apple Business Connect. Resubmit sitemap in Search Console; request indexing for the
three new city pages.

**Weeks 2–3** — Tier 2 citations (six of them), all with the identical NAP. Ask five past
clients for reviews.

**Weeks 4–6** — Clutch / DesignRush / GoodFirms profiles once two reviews are live. Post weekly.
Add 3 photos.

**Weeks 6–10** — Chamber membership if cash allows. Pitch one local news story. Publish the
next city page cluster only if the first three show impressions.
