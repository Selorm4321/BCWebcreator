How It Works

MAKE-TO-ORDER
POLICY

We don't keep inventory. Every order is placed fresh — zero dead stock, zero guesswork, zero risk on your end.

📋 Confirm First

Size, colour, and quantity must be confirmed in writing before we place any blank orders. No assumptions made.

💳 50% Deposit

Required upfront before blanks are ordered. Covers materials so every job is committed before we spend a dollar.

⏱ Turnaround

5–10 business days from deposit received. This allows time to source blanks and complete production.

📦 Blank Sourcing

We order from S&S Activewear, Alphabroder, and Gildan direct. Consistent quality, no middleman markups.

Deposit is non-refundable once blanks are ordered. Cancellations before ordering receive a full refund.

1 free design revision included on all orders. Additional revisions billed at $25/hr.
