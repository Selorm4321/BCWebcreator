E-commerce FAQ

Which platform do you use?

I primarily specialize in WooCommerce (WordPress) for its flexibility and ownership, but I can also build on Shopify depending on your specific business needs.

Is it secure?

Security is non-negotiable. I implement SSL certificates, secure hosting environments, and use payment processors (like Stripe) that handle sensitive data on their own secure servers, so credit card info never touches your database directly.

Can I manage products myself?

Yes. I build stores that are easy to manage. You will be able to add new products, change prices, manage inventory, and process orders through a user-friendly dashboard.

How do shipping costs work?

We can configure shipping in many ways: flat rates, free shipping over a certain amount, or real-time calculations from carriers like Canada Post, UPS, or FedEx.
