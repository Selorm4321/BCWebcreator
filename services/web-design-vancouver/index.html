What Is Custom Web Design?

Custom web design means building your website from the ground up, tailored specifically to your brand story, audience, and business goals. Unlike template-based solutions, custom design gives you complete control over user experience, visual identity, and functionality.

Our Web Design Process

1. Discover Your Story

We start by understanding your business, audience, and goals. What story do you need to tell? Who needs to hear it? What action do you want visitors to take?

2. Visualize Your Brand

Using principles from film production—composition, color theory, visual hierarchy—we design mockups that capture your brand's unique personality while guiding visitors toward conversion.

3. Build Your Digital Presence

Clean, modern code built on proven platforms. Mobile-responsive, fast-loading, and optimized for search engines from day one.

4. Launch & Support

Thorough testing across devices and browsers, then a smooth launch. Ongoing support ensures your site continues performing at its best.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost in Vancouver?

Professional website design in Vancouver typically ranges from $2,500 for a small business site to $8,000+ for complex e-commerce or custom platforms. The exact cost depends on the number of pages, custom features, and project complexity.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical small business website takes 4-6 weeks from contract signing to launch. E-commerce sites require 8-10 weeks. Complex custom platforms take 12+ weeks depending on features.

Do I own my website after it's built?

Yes, absolutely. You own all design files, content, and code. I provide full access to hosting and content management so you're never locked in.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Every website I build is fully mobile-responsive, meaning it automatically adapts to any screen size. Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices, so this is standard practice.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes, I offer monthly maintenance plans starting at $150/month including security updates, backups, content updates, and technical support.
