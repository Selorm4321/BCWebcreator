Surrey and Langley web design questions

My business serves both Surrey and Langley. Do I need separate websites?

No. One well-built site can cover both. We set up your Google Business Profile with the right service area and write your service pages so customers in either city can see you work there, without creating near-identical pages that search engines ignore.

What should a contractor or trades website include?

A page for each main job type, a gallery of finished work, the areas you cover, and a quick way to request a quote. Those four things answer most of what a homeowner or property manager wants to know before they reach out.
