Questions

Frequently asked questions.

A few details that make it easier to get started.

How much does a new website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, features, and integrations you need. Most small business sites fall into an affordable, fixed-price package. Contact us for a free, no-pressure quote.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical brochure or small business site takes 2–4 weeks from design approval to launch. Larger projects with custom features may take longer, and we always keep you informed.

Do you work with businesses outside British Columbia?

Yes. We are based in Abbotsford, BC, and also work remotely with clients across Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom by email and video call.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site we build includes technical SEO fundamentals, fast load times, mobile optimization, and structured data to help search engines understand your content.

Can I update the website myself?

Absolutely. We build sites that are easy to edit and provide training or documentation so you can make updates confidently.
