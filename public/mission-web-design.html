Web Design • Mission, BC

Mission web design built for a town people drive through and stop in

Mission sits on the north bank with the West Coast Express running through it and half the Fraser Valley driving past on the way to somewhere else. A good site here does one job: turns that traffic into a reason to stop, call or book. Small market, so the site has to work harder and cost less.

What we get right for Mission businesses

Right-sized, not overbuilt

Mission is a smaller market. You do not need a twelve-page content programme — you need five pages that answer real questions, load fast and are findable. We quote to that, not to a template package.

The landmarks and the neighbourhoods

Westminster Abbey, the Powerhouse at Stave Falls, Hatzic, Silverdale, Dewdney. We write local content around the names residents and visitors use, because those are the searches.

Commuter and household services

A lot of Mission works elsewhere and shops locally on weekends. For trades and services that means clear hours, a phone number that taps to call, and a quote form that works on a phone.

Tourism and outdoor traffic

Hikers, anglers and day-trippers research before they drive. Directions, access, what to bring and current conditions are content, and they rank better than a slogan.

Who we build for in Mission

Trades, renovation and home services

Service pages that rank individually, job photos that prove the work, and a quote request with the details you need to price it without a call-back.

Tourism, recreation and hospitality

Access, hours, booking and current conditions for anyone whose customer is deciding whether the drive is worth it.

Farms, berries and rural producers

Season, product and pick-your-own information, hours that change weekly, and a way for wholesale buyers to reach you.

Professional and personal services

Clear scope, honest pricing structure and an intake form — the three things that stop a small service business wasting evenings on enquiries that go nowhere.

Mission web design questions

Is Mission too far for an in-person meeting?

No. We are based in Abbotsford, about 20 minutes from Mission across the river, so meeting in person in the District or in Hatzic is straightforward. Plenty of clients still choose to run everything remotely by email and video call.

Do you work with businesses outside the District, like Hatzic or Silverdale?

Yes, and those names belong in the site. We build the local content so a business in Hatzic, Silverdale, Dewdney or Stave Falls can be found by people searching for that area rather than only for Mission as a whole.

Cost, timelines and self-editing are covered in the main FAQ, and the portfolio shows the kind of site you would get.
