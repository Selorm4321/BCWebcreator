Chilliwack web design that works the whole year, not just the season

Chilliwack runs on two calendars: the summer that brings people out to Cultus Lake and the Vedder, and the rest of the year when locals are the ones buying. We build sites that serve both — clear hours and directions for visitors, and enough substance that Google still shows you in February.

What we get right for Chilliwack businesses

Open in the season, closed in the off-season

A tourism or farm site that still says "open daily" in November costs you trust. We set hours, season notices and closures up so one edit changes the whole site, and show you how before launch.

Found in Sardis, Vedder and Promontory, not just "Chilliwack"

People here search by neighbourhood and by landmark. We write and structure pages around the places your customers actually name — Cultus Lake, Yarrow, Rosedale, the Vedder River.

Built for phones on the road

Visitors searching from the highway are on a phone with one bar of signal. Fast pages, tap-to-call and directions that work on a bad connection matter more here than in most towns.

Local suppliers and trades, listed properly

If you install, build, repair or supply, your site needs to show service area, licences, and real job photos. We build service pages per job type so each one can rank on its own.

Chilliwack web design questions

Do you meet in Chilliwack, or is everything remote?

Both work. We are based in Abbotsford, roughly 30 minutes west, so an in-person discovery call or design review in Chilliwack is easy to arrange. Most clients prefer to run the project by email and video and never meet at all.

Can you handle a seasonal business that is only busy half the year?

Yes, and it is a common setup here. We build the site so seasonal hours, closures and "open for the season" notices are editable in one place, and so the content still has substance in the off months instead of reading as an abandoned site.

Cost, timelines and self-editing are covered in the main FAQ, and the portfolio shows the kind of site you would get.
